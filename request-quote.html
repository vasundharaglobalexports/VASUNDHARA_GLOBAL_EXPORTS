FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Granite Export from India

Answers to common questions from international granite buyers.

What granite products do you export from India?

VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS supplies and exports premium Indian granite blocks, gangsaw slabs, cutter slabs, granite tiles and cut-to-size granite for international importers, distributors, wholesalers, fabricators, contractors and project buyers.

Which Indian granite varieties do you supply?

VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS supplies popular Indian granite varieties including Black Galaxy Granite, Absolute Black Granite, Black Markino Granite, Tan Brown Granite, Steel Grey Granite, Viscount White Granite, Himalayan Red Granite and Black Forest Granite. Other granite colours and varieties can be sourced depending on specifications and availability.

Are you an Indian granite supplier and exporter?

Yes. VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS is an Indian granite supplier and exporter serving international B2B buyers with premium granite blocks, slabs, tiles and cut-to-size natural stone from India.

Which countries do you export Indian granite to?

VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS serves international granite buyers across the USA, Canada, Europe, Middle East, Asia and other global markets. Shipping arrangements depend on the destination, product and order requirements.

Can I buy granite slabs, blocks and tiles directly from India?

Yes. International buyers can source Indian granite slabs, granite blocks and granite tiles according to the required granite variety, dimensions, thickness, finish, quantity and destination.

Do you supply wholesale, bulk and regular B2B granite orders?

Yes. VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS supports importers, distributors, wholesalers, fabricators, contractors and project buyers requiring wholesale, bulk, project or repeat granite supply from India.

What granite finishes and cut-to-size options are available?

Depending on the granite variety, VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS can supply polished, honed, flamed, leathered and project-specific granite finishes. Cut-to-size granite can be supplied according to buyer drawings, dimensions, thickness and project specifications.

Can buyers inspect granite before shipment?

Yes. VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS can provide product photographs, videos and relevant inspection information before dispatch, helping international buyers review the granite material, finish and specifications before shipment.

How are granite packing, container loading and export shipment handled?

VASUNDHARA GLOBAL EXPORTS coordinates export-ready granite packaging and container loading with attention to material protection, suitable crates, bundles or frames, weight distribution and relevant shipment documentation.

How can I get an Indian granite export quotation?

Use our Request Export Quotation form and provide the granite variety or product, size, thickness, finish, quantity and destination country or port. Drawings, packing preferences and inspection requirements can also be provided when applicable.
